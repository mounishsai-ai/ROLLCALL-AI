01 — The Problem & The Idea

Roll call doesn't scale

Calling names by voice or passing a sheet costs real class time every session, and it's easy to mark a friend present who isn't in the room. A single group photo already contains the truth — nobody was using it.

Let one photo do the work

Photograph the room once. The system finds every face, matches it against the roster, and for anyone it isn't sure about, it investigates — the same way a teacher would lean in for a second look — before it commits to an answer.

02 — What Makes This Different

Most face-attendance tools return a silent yes or no. This one shows its reasoning — live, per face — and is honest when it changes its mind.

THE ADJUDICATOR — an agentic loop, not a single-pass classifier

Every face that isn't an easy match gets re-cropped at higher resolution, re-embedded, and — if it's still unclear — shown to a vision model alongside a short list of the closest candidates on the roster, which is asked to pick one or say none. Corrections are flagged, not hidden. Faces that belong to nobody enrolled are reported as strangers, never silently assigned to whoever scored highest.

03 — Methodology

From one photo to a signed register

1

Detect

RetinaFace locates every face in the photo, regardless of angle, distance, or partial occlusion.

2

Embed

Each face crop is turned into a numeric signature by ArcFace or AdaFace — the same family of models behind production face-ID systems.

3

Search

FAISS compares that signature against every enrolled student by cosine similarity and returns the closest matches in milliseconds.

4

Classify

Each face lands in one of three tiers — confident, uncertain, or unrecognized — based on how convincingly it matched.

5

Adjudicate

Uncertain and unrecognized faces are investigated: re-cropped, re-measured, and — if still unresolved — shown to Gemini Vision with a shortlist.

6

Verdict

Every face reaches present, open-for-review, or absent — each with the reasoning that produced it, ready for a teacher to sign off.

04 — Key Features

One photo, whole class

No queueing, no per-student scan — one capture covers the room.

Visible reasoning

Every uncertain face's investigation is shown live, not buried in a log file.

Handles the hard cases

Blur, angle, distance, and partial occlusion get a second look instead of a shrug.

Reports strangers

Unenrolled faces are surfaced, never quietly folded into the nearest match.

Correction-aware

When a second opinion changes the answer, the register marks it as a correction.

Teacher has the last word

Anything left open is handed to a human, clearly separated from settled attendance.
